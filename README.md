# Lab-on-a-Chip and Artificial Intelligence

This repository organizes research on the intersection of Lab-on-a-Chip (LOC) technology, artificial intelligence (AI), and healthcare cybersecurity.

## Research overview

The source paper discusses how combining LOC devices with AI and machine learning (ML) could support automated diagnostics, image analysis, predictive analytics, personalized treatment, and remote patient monitoring. It also considers cybersecurity risks associated with connecting these devices to healthcare systems, focusing on data breaches, unauthorized access, and hardware tampering.

The paper describes safeguards including encryption, stronger authentication, security audits, tamper-evident packaging, supply-chain checks, and ongoing monitoring. These are topics covered by the paper, not independently validated findings or implementation guidance from this repository.

## Scope and source note

The summary above reflects the paper's stated topics and perspective. It does not claim that the paper's examples or projected benefits have been independently reproduced or verified. Any subsequent analysis, code, or conclusions added here should identify its data sources and methods.

The source paper is:

> Abuarqob, Esra. “The Future of Healthcare: Advancements and Cybersecurity Challenges in Lab-on-a-Chip Technology.” Coastal Carolina University, June 29, 2024.
